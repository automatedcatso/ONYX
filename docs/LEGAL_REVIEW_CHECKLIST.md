# Legal Review Checklist

The in-product policy pages are technical templates, not legal advice. Before public launch, obtain qualified review for the operating jurisdiction and campus rules.

Review:

- Operating entity and role-based contact details
- Eligibility and age requirements
- Prohibited goods and regulated categories
- Privacy notice, provider disclosures, and lawful bases
- Data retention, deletion, safety holds, and law-enforcement requests
- AI disclosure and human review
- Grievance, appeal, and moderator accountability procedures
- Consumer protection, disclaimers, and limitation of liability
- Accessibility obligations
- Incident and breach notification duties
- Copyright, trademark, and user-content complaints
- Campus approval and acceptable-use requirements

Record the approved policy version and effective date. Update the UI templates before production use.
